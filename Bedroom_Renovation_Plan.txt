Bedroom Renovation Plan
 
Goal: 
Refresh a small bedroom on a low budget, 
make it brighter and more functional.

Constraints: 
1500PLN, 2 weekends.
 
Priorities: 
High: patch walls, repaint (2 coats), fix flooring.
Medium: new skirting boards, rearrange furniture.
Low: new decorations.

Plan:
1. Measure room, set budget, write shopping list and checklist.
2. Clear small items, cover remaining furniture with foil or fabric.
3. Remove baseboards/nails, patch cracks, holes and sand them.
4. Paint ceiling, then walls (2 coats, dry between).
5. Repair flooring, add new skirting boards.
6. Assemble furniture, check for missed repairs and paint splatters.
7. Clean, vacuum, dust, wash windows, place furniture and decorations.

Risks: hidden wall damage might create additional costs, 
Paint might take longer to dry, start at least a day earlier.
